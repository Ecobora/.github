## What We Build

We operate at the intersection of hardware and software — connecting physical energy assets to intelligent software systems that make data actionable.

| Product | Description | Stack |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| **Emissionfy** | IoT energy monitoring & carbon emissions tracking platform | Next.js · TypeScript · MQTT |
| **Emissionfy IoT** | Smart meter firmware and device management layer | C++ · MQTT · NB-IoT |
| **ecobora.co** | Company platform and client-facing portal | Next.js · TypeScript |

---

## Our Focus Areas

**Energy Monitoring**
Real-time visibility into energy consumption across solar, grid, and hybrid installations. From a single meter to an entire facility.

**Carbon & Emissions Tracking**
Automated carbon footprint calculation from live energy data. Built to support ESG reporting and sustainability compliance.

**IoT Device Management**
End-to-end device lifecycle management — from SIM provisioning and firmware updates to telemetry ingestion and alerting.

**Smart Infrastructure**
Custom hardware integrations and embedded firmware for smart meters, sensors, and energy controllers.
